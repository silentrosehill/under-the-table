## Sound & music

No audio files. Every sound (room tone, the bulb's hum, the record's crackle, the jazz trio on it,
dice, paper, ice, the lighter, the typewriter keys) is synthesized live in `public/sfx.js` with the
Web Audio API, so there is nothing to credit or license. The music is generated, not a recording.
The roulette, card stings and casino event sounds in `public/sfx.js` are synthesised with Web Audio too (no audio files).

## Interface icons

The line icons in the game's toolbar are drawn inline in `public/index.html` (original, no library).

## Cards & casino art

All card faces (`public/cardface.js`, `public/cardface.css`) and the 17 illustrations in `public/art/cards/*.svg`
(`billionaire`, `bribe`, `casino-bombing`, `casino-boom`, `casino-gang`, `casino-mayor`, `casino-onetoomany`, `casino-police`, `casino-whim`,
`crime`, `gang`, `gift`, `mafia-note`, `mafia-table`, `protection`, `squatters`, `squatters_in`) are original work made for this project:
hand-authored SVG and CSS, no third-party assets. License: same as the project (original work).

City Mode's casino (`public/city/casino.js`) and tile effects (`public/city/tilefx.js`) are procedural, original code
with no third-party assets, under the same license as the project.
