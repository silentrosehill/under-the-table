## Sound & music

No audio files. Every sound (room tone, the bulb's hum, the record's crackle, the jazz trio on it,
dice, paper, ice, the lighter, the typewriter keys) is synthesized live in `public/sfx.js` with the
Web Audio API, so there is nothing to credit or license. The music is generated, not a recording.
The roulette, card stings and casino event sounds in `public/sfx.js` are synthesised with Web Audio too (no audio files).

## Interface icons

The line icons in the game's toolbar are drawn inline in `public/index.html` (original, no library).
